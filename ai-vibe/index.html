VoxelGrove

A tiny floating-island sandbox

How to play

  • WASDMove
  • MouseLook around
  • SpaceJump
  • Left clickMine / attack
  • Right clickBuild block
  • 1–5Choose block
  • QSwitch tool
  • EscRelease mouse / pause

Mossling and Stoneguard monsters roam the island — strike them when they're close (they hit back!). Mining and building take about a second; hold the button to keep going. Your island saves automatically.

You were defeated

Your island is safe — every block you mined or built is still saved.

Reset the island?

All of your building changes will be erased and the original island (seed 42) will be restored. This can't be undone.
